
# DataCo Global Sales and Customer Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a two-page Sales and Customer Intelligence Dashboard developed for DataCo Global.

The dashboard provides an overview of the company's sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance, regional sales, and delivery operations.

The objective of this project is to transform sales data into meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making, improve operational efficiency, and identify opportunities for business growth.

## Business Questions Addressed

This dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:

- How much revenue and profit does the business generate?
- How has sales performance changed compared with the previous year?
- Which markets contribute the most to sales?
- Which customer segments generate the highest sales?
- Which products generate the highest profits?
- Which departments contribute most to profitability?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which regions have the highest late delivery risk?
- How long does it take to deliver orders on average?

## Key Business Findings

1. Total sales reached 36.78M, with total profits of 3.97M.
2. Consumer customers account for 51.8% of the customer segment distribution and generate 19.1M in sales.
3. Europe, LATAM, and USCA each display sales of 9,141.42 in the top market chart.
4. Web Camera is the highest-ranked product by profit, generating 30,290.
5. Golf contributes the highest profit among the displayed departments, at 497,524.
6. The sales trend shows a sharp decline in 2018 that requires further investigation.
7. US Center and West Asia have the highest displayed late delivery risk values, at 19 each.
8. Average delivery time is 3 days, with no change compared with the previous year.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the sharp decline in 2018 sales to determine whether it reflects incomplete data, reduced sales activity, or a change in business performance.
- Develop customer retention and repeat-purchase campaigns for the Consumer segment.
- Investigate the identical sales values displayed for Europe, LATAM, and USCA to confirm the accuracy of the market figures.
- Analyze the factors contributing to Web Camera's strong profitability and identify opportunities to improve the performance of other products.
- Review the Golf department's product mix and profitability to identify opportunities for sustainable growth.
- Investigate late delivery risk in US Center and West Asia and review the operational factors contributing to delays.
- Monitor sales, profit, and delivery performance regularly to identify emerging business risks.

## Project Conclusion

The DataCo Global Sales and Customer Intelligence Dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and delivery operations.

The analysis highlights opportunities to strengthen customer retention, improve product profitability, investigate changes in sales performance, and reduce delivery risks.

The project demonstrates how business intelligence dashboards can support data-driven decision-making by translating business data into actionable insights.
